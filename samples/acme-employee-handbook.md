# Acme Robotics — Employee Handbook (2026 edition)

This handbook is fictional sample data for the Recall demo. It describes the
policies that apply to all full-time employees of Acme Robotics Pvt. Ltd.

## 1. Working hours

Core working hours are 10:00 to 16:00 IST, Monday to Friday. Outside core
hours, employees may choose when to work, provided they complete 40 hours per
week. Hours are recorded in the TimeTrack portal by 18:00 every Friday.

## 2. Leave

### 2.1 Annual leave
Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar
year. Leave accrues at 2 days per month. Up to 10 unused days may be carried
over into the next year; any days above 10 are forfeited on 31 March.

### 2.2 Sick leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is
required for sick leave longer than 3 consecutive days. Sick leave does not
carry over.

### 2.3 Parental leave
Birthing parents receive 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Non-birthing parents
receive 6 weeks of paid parental leave, which must be taken within 12 months of
the child's birth or adoption.

## 3. Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with their manager's
approval. Fully remote arrangements require approval from the department head
and HR. Remote workers must be reachable on Slack during core hours.

The company reimburses home-office equipment up to INR 25,000 once every two
years. Claims are submitted through the Expenses portal with an itemised
receipt.

## 4. Travel and expenses

Domestic travel is booked through the TravelDesk portal at least 7 days in
advance. Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours. The daily meal
allowance during business travel is INR 1,500 in metro cities and INR 1,000
elsewhere.

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Claims
above INR 50,000 require approval from a director.

## 5. Learning and development

Each employee has an annual learning budget of INR 40,000 for courses,
certifications, books, and conferences. Unused budget does not carry over.
Employees may also take 5 paid learning days per year for approved training.

## 6. IT and security

Laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after 5 minutes of
inactivity. Passwords must be at least 14 characters long, and multi-factor
authentication is mandatory for all company systems.

Security incidents, including lost devices, must be reported to the security
team at security@acme.example within 1 hour of discovery. The internal
incident code for a lost or stolen laptop is SEC-LOST-01.

## 7. Performance reviews

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Each review
includes a self-assessment, manager feedback, and goals for the next cycle.
Salary revisions are effective from 1 July.

## 8. Notice period

The notice period is 30 days during probation and 60 days after confirmation.
Probation lasts 6 months from the joining date.
